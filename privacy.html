Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

GTA6 Shorts Auto is a tool for preparing and publishing short-form gaming videos to TikTok accounts authorized by their owners.

Information We Process

When a user authorizes TikTok access, the application may process account identifiers, authorization tokens, video files, captions, and publishing status.

How Information Is Used

Information is used to authenticate the user, upload authorized videos, and track publishing results. We do not sell personal information.

Storage and Security

Authorization credentials and publishing records are intended to be stored locally on the user's computer. Users should protect access to their computer and credentials.

Data Deletion

Users can revoke application access through their TikTok account settings and request deletion of locally stored application data from the application operator.

Contact

For privacy questions or data deletion requests, contact the application operator through the contact information provided with the application.
